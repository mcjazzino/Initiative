# Initiative

A phone companion for D&D combat. Initiative helps you through your turn: what you can still do with your action, bonus action, reaction and movement, which spells and features you have left, and what changed when the fight is over.

**Open it:** https://mcjazzino.github.io/initiative/

- Works for D&D 5e (2014) and 5.5e (2024), every class from level 1 to 20.
- Bring your character in from D&D Beyond, build it from your sheet (Roll20, paper, anything), or pick a ready-made character.
- Your character sheet stays the source of truth. At the end of combat, Initiative tells you what to update on it.
- Everything is saved in your own browser. No account, no tracking. Clearing your browser data removes your character.
